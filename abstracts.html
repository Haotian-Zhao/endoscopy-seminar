1. Tori and conjugacy in SL(2)

Speaker: Chen Cheng · November 3, 2026

The theory of endoscopy provides a framework for studying certain instances of Langlands functoriality. I will give a brief overview of the main themes of the seminar based on the framework of the endoscopy. Then we turn to the SL_2 case for details, focusing on the torus and the conjugacy classes.

2. Restriction from GL(2) to SL(2)

Speaker: Zhao Haotian · November 10, 2026

Following Dr. Chen Cheng's introduction to tori and conjugacy classes in SL(2), I will turn to the representation side. The main question is how an irreducible admissible representation of GL(2,F) decomposes upon restriction to SL(2,F). I will explain why the restriction is multiplicity-free, how self-twists determine the number of irreducible constituents, and how this relates to L-indistinguishability and L-packets for SL(2).

3. Whittaker Models and Self-Twists for SL(2)

Speaker: Zhao Haotian · November 17, 2026 (9:00–10:00 a.m. HKT)

Continuing our study of the restriction from GL(2) to SL(2), I will first complete the multiplicity-one argument from last time. We will then investigate how the Whittaker models of an irreducible representation of SL(2) are related to its stabilizer under conjugation by GL(2). Finally, we will introduce the notion of self-twists and explain how they determine the number of irreducible constituents in the restriction. The discussion follows Corollary 2.7 and Lemma 2.8 of Labesse–Langlands’ L-indistinguishability for SL(2).

Endoscopy Seminar: Reading Assignments

Back to the seminar homepage

The following reading assignments and topics to cover are provisional. References are abbreviated as [L] Langlands, [LL] Labesse–Langlands, [K] Kaletha, and [P] Parab. Links to the texts are on the seminar homepage.

1. Tori and conjugacy in SL(2)

Primary: [L], Chapter I, §3. Supplement: [K], §2.2.

Why consider SL(2)? Split and elliptic tori; rational and stable conjugacy in an explicit example.

2. Restriction from GL(2) to SL(2)

Primary: [LL], §2, Lemmas 2.4–2.8.

Finite decomposition, multiplicity one, and the relation with self-twists.

3. L-packets and characters

Primary: [L], Chapter IV, §2. Supplements: [L], Chapter IV, §1; the representation-theoretic part of [LL], §2.

Concrete packets and their characters, with examples over real and nonarchimedean fields.

4. Orbital integrals and stability

Primary: [L], Chapter I, §4; [LL], §2, from the beginning to Lemma 2.1. Supplement: [K], §§2.2 and 3.1.

Ordinary, stable, and κ-orbital integrals; the sum and difference within a two-class stable conjugacy class.

5. Endoscopic tori for SL(2)

Primary: [L], Chapter II, §§1 and 3–4. Supplement: [K], §§3.2–3.4.

The norm-one torus and its endoscopic datum. General definitions will be illustrated in this case.

6. Transfer factors

Primary: [L], Chapter III, §§1–3, focusing on SL(2). Supplement: [K], §§2.5 and 3.5.

The explicit SL(2) factor and the roles of the discriminant, sign, and normalization.

7. Endoscopic character identities

Primary: the character-transfer discussion in the latter part of [LL], §2. Supplement: [K], §2.5 (especially (2.5.2)) and §3.6.

Transfer from a torus and the corresponding weighted combination of characters in a packet.

8. Local transfer and the fundamental lemma

Primary: [LL], §2, proof of Lemma 2.1 and Definitions 2.2–2.3. Supplement: [K], §3.6 (especially Theorem 3.6.4).

An explicit local-transfer calculation; the statement and role of the unit fundamental lemma.

9. The trace formula for compact quotients

Primary: [P], §2, the co-compact case. Supplement: [L], Chapter I, §6, for orientation.

The integral kernel and its geometric and spectral expansions in the compact-quotient case.

10. Truncation and continuous spectrum

Primary: [P], §§3–4, with selected formulas and explanations in §§6–8. Supplement: [K], §6.3.

Divergence, truncation, and the continuous spectrum; the structure of the resulting expansions.

11. Stabilization of the regular elliptic term

Primary: [L], Chapter I, §6; [K], §§2.3–2.5.

Rational and adelic conjugacy classes, endoscopic torus terms, and the use of local transfer.

12. The stable trace formula

Primary: selected parts of [K], §§6.4–6.5. Supplement: [L], Chapter I, §7.

An overview of the remaining terms and the spectral interpretation in terms of packets and multiplicities.

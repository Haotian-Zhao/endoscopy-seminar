1. Tori and conjugacy in SL(2)

Speaker: Chen Cheng · November 3, 2026

The theory of endoscopy provides a framework for studying certain instances of Langlands functoriality. I will give a brief overview of the main themes of the seminar based on the framework of the endoscopy. Then we turn to the SL_2 case for details, focusing on the torus and the conjugacy classes.

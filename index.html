Student Number Theory Seminar: Endoscopy

This is a student reading seminar in number theory and representation theory, centered on endoscopy for SL(2). Talks are intended to be accessible to graduate students, with time for questions and discussion. Everyone interested is welcome.
We will meet every other Tuesday, 10:00 a.m.–12:00 noon (Hong Kong time), beginning November 3, 2026. In Berkeley, this is Monday 6:00–8:00 p.m. (PST), shifting to 7:00–9:00 p.m. (PDT) from March 2027. The classroom is to be confirmed; Zoom participation will be arranged for participants joining from Berkeley.
If you would like to be added to the seminar mailing list, please contact Haotian Zhao.

2026–27 topic: Endoscopy for SL(2).

Prerequisites: Familiarity with local fields and basic representation theory is helpful. Some background in reductive groups and local class field theory may also be useful.

Main references:

  • Robert P. Langlands, Les débuts d'une formule des traces stable (1983). [L]
  • Jean-Pierre Labesse and Robert P. Langlands, L-indistinguishability for SL(2), Canadian Journal of Mathematics 31 (1979), 726–785. [LL]

Other references:

  • Tasho Kaletha, Lectures on the Stable Trace Formula with Emphasis on SL(2), NUS lecture notes. [K]
  • Abhishek Parab, Introductory Course on the Stable Trace Formula, with Emphasis on SL(2), NUS lecture notes (2018). [P]
